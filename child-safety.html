Feel Live Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Feel Live is an adults-only live social community. Users must be 18 or older. These standards apply to profiles, messages, live rooms, uploaded media, and all other activity on Feel Live.

Prohibited content and conduct

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material, sexual solicitation or grooming of minors, sexualisation of minors, trafficking, and attempts to share or obtain abusive content. Content that promotes, facilitates, or normalises these activities is prohibited.

Report a concern

Use the Report option in a user's profile or live room, or contact infofeellive@gmail.com. Include the user ID, room ID, approximate time, and a description. Do not send, download, or redistribute suspected child sexual abuse material.

Enforcement

We review reports, remove prohibited content, restrict or disable accounts involved in exploitation, and take steps to prevent further abuse. We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the appropriate regional or national authority as required by applicable law and cooperate with lawful requests.

Child safety contact

Our designated child safety contact can be reached at infofeellive@gmail.com.
